# ADR-XXXX — <Short noun phrase naming the decision>

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-XXXX
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play? What makes this a decision rather than an obvious default?

Write this so it makes sense to someone — including you — who has forgotten everything about the current state of the project. Facts and constraints, not opinions; the opinion goes in Decision.

If the decision is expensive to reverse, say so here.

## Options considered

**Option A.** What it is. What it gives you. What it costs.

**Option B.** As above.

**Option C.** As above.

List the options you genuinely weighed, including any you rejected quickly — a rejected option with one line explaining why is more useful than an unmentioned one. Two options is thin; more than five usually means the decision hasn't been framed properly yet.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly and in the active voice: *"Use X."* — not *"It was decided that X might be appropriate."*

Then the reasoning: why this option beat the others, given the context above.

If the decision only holds under certain conditions, name them.

## Consequences

**Good:** what becomes easier or possible.

**Costs:** what becomes harder, slower, or permanently closed off.

**Accepted risks:** what could go wrong that you are choosing to live with, and roughly what you would do if it did.

This section is mandatory and it is the most valuable part of the file. A decision with no stated cost has not been thought through — every real choice gives something up. If you cannot name the cost, you have probably not compared the options seriously.
